Not a conventional GC

We don't compete with your builder. We clear the way for them.

Nexora isn't bidding against your general contractor — we're the specialist crew GCs, developers, and property managers call in before ground gets broken. Asbestos survey came back positive. There's a structure that needs to come down. The soil needs to be tested. The ground isn't stable enough to build on yet.

We take on the regulated, high-stakes part of the project — the part that stalls timelines when it's handled by someone without the licensing or experience — and hand back a site that's documented, compliant, and ready for the next trade.

How a site gets from "problem" to "ready"

Five stages. One point of contact.

01

Survey

02

03

Demolish

Full and selective demolition, clearance, and disposal coordination.

04

Stabilize
